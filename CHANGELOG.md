# Changelog

All notable changes to Hopla. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com),
and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [0.1.0] — first public version

### Added
- A code-drawn, articulated creature that pops up in a corner of the screen with no window, offers
  **Go** / **Later**, does the moves with you, celebrates and disappears.
- 55 creatures in 11 collections (an art style + a creature family), with unique hands and feet.
- 18 basic moves (11 seated, 7 standing) and 10 pre-recorded routes for each session length
  (30 s, 45 s, 1 min, 2 min), rotating at every visit, daily, weekly, monthly or never.
- A 3-second pause to stand up or sit down between seated and standing moves.
- Settings window: avatar gallery and editor, session, exercise list, rhythm, progress calendar, general.
- Sounds synthesized on the fly, one voice per collection.
- Do not disturb during calls and screen sharing; time away from the Mac counts as a break.
- French and English, global shortcuts, launch at login, "Reduce motion" support.
